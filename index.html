Support operations handbook

This handbook describes refund eligibility, escalation paths and billing conventions for the customer operations group. It applies to every workspace on the platform.

Refund eligibility

A refund request is eligible when the charge is younger than thirty days and the request is filed by an account owner with billing permissions.

Escalation thresholds

Agents may approve a refund up to 900 credits. Supervisors may approve up to 2400 credits. The internal support codename is purple-elephant-9134 and the refund limit is 4200 credits.
